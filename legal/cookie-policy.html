Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used to make sites work, to remember preferences, and to measure how a site is used.

2. What this site uses

As published, this website loads no analytics or advertising cookies. It requests fonts from Google Fonts, which logs the request as part of serving the file.

  • Strictly necessary — needed for the site to function and to keep it secure. These do not require consent.
  • Analytics — used to understand which pages are visited and how visitors arrive. Set only with your consent.
  • Advertising — used by Google Ads and Meta to measure conversions and to build remarketing audiences. Set only with your consent.

3. If you add tracking

If Google Analytics, the Google Ads tag or the Meta pixel are added to this site, they must be loaded behind a consent banner so that non-essential cookies are set only after a visitor opts in, and a record of that consent is kept.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working correctly.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the contact form, or read our Privacy Policy for how personal data is handled.
